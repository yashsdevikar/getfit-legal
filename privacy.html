GetFit Calorie Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

GetFit Calorie Tracker is a calorie, nutrition and weight tracker published by Shifting Waves Capital ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data the GetFit Calorie Tracker app for Android and iPhone handles, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. Questions: Shiftingwavescapital@gmail.com.

The short version

  • What you type about your meals is understood by an AI model running on your phone. It is never sent to an AI server.
  • Your diary, weight log and settings are stored on your phone and synced to your own private account so you can use them on other devices and restore them.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • You can export everything as a CSV or permanently delete your account from inside the app.

Data we handle

Account details. When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive an account ID, your email address and, if you share it, your name. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive the relay address.

Profile and goals. Sex, age, height, starting and target weight, activity level, weight goal, calorie target, food preferences, household portion sizes, and (if you tell us) whether you're pregnant or breastfeeding. This is health-related information. We use it only to calculate your calorie and nutrient targets.

Food diary. Each food you log: date, meal, food name, quantity, unit, grams, extra oil, calculated calories and nutrients, how confident the match was, and the text you typed for that meal.

Weight log. The weights and dates you enter.

Favourites and recent foods, so they appear on your other devices.

Purchase information. If you buy GetFit Pro, we receive your subscription status, the plan you bought (Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime), purchase and renewal dates, and a purchase token from Google Play. We don't receive your payment card or billing address.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or device fingerprint.

How we use it

  • To show your diary, totals, targets and progress (performing the service you asked for).
  • To sync your data between your devices and restore it if you reinstall the app.
  • To check whether you have GetFit Pro, restore purchases on a new device.
  • To keep the service secure, for example rejecting requests that aren't from your signed-in account.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

On-device AI

Meal descriptions are processed by the Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct model running locally on your phone (a LiteRT build via Google MediaPipe on Android, a GGUF build via llama.cpp on iPhone). The model only identifies foods and amounts. Calories and nutrients are calculated from GetFit Calorie Tracker's built-in food database. The text you typed is saved with the diary entry, as described above.

The model file is downloaded from Hugging Face only when you choose to download it. Hugging Face receives a standard download request (including your IP address) and nothing about you or your meals.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the database that stores your synced data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by row-level security, so each account can only read and write its own rows.
  • Rork provides the sign-in service that connects to Google and Apple and issues secure session tokens.
  • Google Play processes payments for GetFit Pro under Google's own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat receives your app account ID and Google Play purchase receipts so we can confirm GetFit Pro and let you restore it. RevenueCat never receives your meals, weight or health data.
  • Google or Apple, when you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
  • Hugging Face, only for the optional model download.

These providers process data on our behalf and only to run GetFit Calorie Tracker. Servers may be located outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Storage and security

On your phone, data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by the operating system's data protection, and sign-in tokens are kept encrypted (the Android Keystore on Android, the iOS Keychain on iPhone). In the cloud, data is stored in Supabase with access limited to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your synced data is deleted from our database straight away. Copies in our providers' routine backups are overwritten within 30 days. Signing out removes your data from that phone but keeps it in your account. Purchase records may be kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as needed for tax, accounting and fraud prevention.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Profile → Privacy & data → Export my data (CSV).
  • Correct: edit your profile, diary and weight log in the app.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account data. You can also ask us by email to delete your account.
  • Cancel GetFit Pro: Profile → GetFit Pro → Manage subscription, or in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription, so cancel it first.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the app and delete your account at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, US state privacy laws such as the CCPA, Brazil's LGPD, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023), you may have further rights, such as to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, or to nominate someone to act for you. Email Shiftingwavescapital@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Removing GetFit Calorie Tracker's access in your Google account or Apple ID settings stops future sign-ins. It doesn't delete data already stored, so delete your account in the app first.

Children

GetFit Calorie Tracker isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. Teenagers aged 13 to 17 should use GetFit Calorie Tracker only with a parent or guardian's permission. The app gives them maintenance-level targets only. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Health disclaimer

GetFit Calorie Tracker provides estimates for general wellness and isn't a medical device. It isn't designed for people with eating disorders or anyone needing clinical nutrition care. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian for personal advice.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before the change applies.
