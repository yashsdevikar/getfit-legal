Delete your GetFit Calorie Tracker account

GetFit Calorie Tracker is published by Shifting Waves Capital. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, in either of these ways.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  • Open GetFit Calorie Tracker and sign in.
  • Go to Profile.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email Shiftingwavescapital@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with (Google or Apple), with the subject Delete my GetFit account. We confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (account ID, email address and name)
  • Your profile and goals (age, sex, height, weights, activity level, calorie target, food preferences)
  • Your food diary, including the meal text you typed
  • Your weight log, favourites and recent foods
  • All data stored on that phone

What may be kept

  • Copies in our providers' routine backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat may be kept as required for tax, accounting and fraud prevention.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in the app (Profile → GetFit Pro → Cancel membership) or in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.
