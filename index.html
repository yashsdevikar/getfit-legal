GetFit Calorie Tracker Support

GetFit Calorie Tracker is published by Shifting Waves Capital. For help, billing questions or data requests, email Shiftingwavescapital@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days.

Manage or cancel GetFit Pro

In the app: Profile → GetFit Pro → Manage subscription. Or in the Google Play Store: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Restore a purchase

Sign in with the same account, then Profile → GetFit Pro → Restore purchases.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your synced diary, weight log and profile. You can also email Shiftingwavescapital@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account within 30 days. Cancel any Google Play subscription first; deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
